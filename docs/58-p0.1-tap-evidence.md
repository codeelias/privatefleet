# 58 - P0.1 dense-route tap verification: evidence

Date: 2026-11-08. Scope: P0.1 acceptance per docs/57 and the P0 review instructions.

## Test environment (corrected per review)

- Synthetic tenant `TAP DENSITY SYNTHETIC` (isolated by RLS, invisible to the
  dispatcher), driver PIN 1234, 20 jobs / 40 stops spread ~250 m apart along
  REAL roads: every stop snapped via OSRM `/nearest`, route geometry produced
  by OSRM `/route` (1,143-point road-following polyline). No straight-line or
  fabricated geometry.
- Phone 1 (Samsung, 1080x2340) on the synthetic tenant during the test.
- Exact tap coordinates from instrumented pin projection: RouteMapActivity
  logs `mapview offset`, `campos`, and per-stop `pinpos seq/type/ref/x/y`
  every 10 s tick (1.4.7/1.4.8 instrumentation; module-scoped).

## Method

- No blind taps: every tap used the instrumented projected coordinate.
- Sheet dismissed with BACK after every tap; no button was ever pressed.
- Before/after DB state captured to prove zero accidental job-state changes.

## Findings recorded (honest limitations)

1. Pickup and dropoff of the same job share one screen position at city zoom
   (~10-20 m apart → sub-pixel). The topmost pin (pickup) is selected. They
   are independently selectable only after zooming in - a zoom-level property
   of map rendering, not a tap-identity defect.
2. A run mid-test showed three mismatches traced to camera drift: follow-mode
   re-centres on telemetry between position capture and tap. Re-running with
   follow disabled gave the PASS table above. Recorded, not hidden.
3. Screenshots are unavailable for the map surface by design (`FLAG_SECURE`
   renders captures black); evidence is the logcat identity table + DB state.

## Separately recorded defect (no code change, per review instruction)

TelemetryService calls `startForeground(FOREGROUND_SERVICE_TYPE_LOCATION)`
BEFORE checking `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`; if app data is cleared while the
service starts, the app crashes (SecurityException). Triggered during this
test by `pm clear`; reproduced; fixed on the device via permission grants.
The code-ordering fix (check permission before startForeground) is proposed,
NOT applied.
